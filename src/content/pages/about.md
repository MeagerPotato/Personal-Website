I'm Allen, an aerospace engineering student at UC Berkeley (class of 2030). I want to work on
autonomous flight, robotics and spacecraft systems, and on the AI that runs them.

At AFRL I took a staged-recovery rocket through eleven design iterations to a 3,200-foot flight. At
Berkeley I'm building an ESP32 telemetry and black-box system on Cal Aero SAE's avionics team and
running logistics for Cal Hacks 13.0.

## Berkeley

I'm on Cal Aero SAE's avionics team, where I built a motor test jig that measures thrust to choose
the motors for our competition RC plane, and I'm designing an ESP32 telemetry and black-box system
that corrects for turbulence through the propellers' power. I'm also logistics director at
[Hackathons @ Berkeley](/projects/hackathons-at-berkeley/): catering, hacker buses, and
merchandise for [Cal Hacks 13.0](/projects/cal-hacks-13/), with more than 3,000 attendees. And
since October 2026 I've been a student lab assistant in Prof. Yvette Fisher's lab, a neuroscience
lab studying how fruit fly compass neurons recalibrate from what the fly sees: I keep the fly
stocks and help with the engineering, from 3D printing and microcontrollers to building rigs.

## Rockets

I spent the summer of 2024 as an engineer intern with the Air Force Research Laboratory in
Albuquerque, working as a workshop and museum assistant at Explora Science Center and Kirtland Air
Force Base on machining, museum design, and prototypes built with FDM and resin printers, laser
cutting, and a CNC lathe and mill. On the side I took a
[staged-recovery rocket](/projects/model-rocketry/) through eleven design iterations; it reached
3,200 feet on its barometric altimeter and came back under its parachute. At school I founded and
captained an American Rocketry Challenge team.

## Robots

In 2023 I co-founded my high school's first [VEX team](/projects/robotics/), eleven of us, and
became its lead programmer. I wrote the autonomous navigation in C++: odometry, sensor fusion,
motion profiling, and Monte Carlo localization, so the robot could work out where it was and get where it was going. The
program grew to seven teams and more than 40 members in 2025, and by 2026 to fourteen teams and more
than 90 members. Across the program: four VEX Worlds qualifications, and National Division Finalist
twice.

## Security

Four years of [CyberPatriot](/projects/cyberpatriot/), as my team's Windows specialist: second place
at the National Finals twice, in 2025 and 2026, and first place in the Cisco Networking Challenge
both years. From 2024 I also taught it, as a Windows instructor for middle and high school students
in my school's program.

## Software

Most of my software orbits [Software](/systems/software/), one of the two suns of
[Projects](/projects/): [FishAI](/projects/fishai/), bots for
[Canadian Fish](/projects/canadian-fish-demo/) (a six-player card game) and the lab that measures
them, and [Days2Meet](/projects/days2meet/), a scheduling poll for the plans where hours are
meaningless. At the [Corgi Hackathon](/projects/corgi/) during Y Combinator's Startup School in
2026 I built RunItBack in twelve hours, a tool that turns a single photo into an interactive 3D
environment for dementia reminiscence therapy, and placed 5th of more than 1,000 competitors.

## Work

At Raytheon in the summer of 2025, as a systems engineering intern, I reverse-engineered an
Arduino-based robotic system and produced its technical documentation, wiring diagrams, and CAD
models, while shadowing the mechanical, electrical, software, and systems teams.
