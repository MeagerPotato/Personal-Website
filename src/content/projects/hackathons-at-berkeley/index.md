Hackathons @ Berkeley is the student team that runs Cal Hacks. As its logistics director I handle
the parts of a hackathon nobody notices until they go wrong: the catering for
[Cal Hacks 13.0](/projects/cal-hacks-13/) and its more than 3,000 attendees, the hacker buses that
bring them, and the event's merchandise, sourced end to end from vendor quotes to delivery.
