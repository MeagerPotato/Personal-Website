I like rockets that come back.

## AFRL Scholars, summer 2024

As an AFRL Scholars intern in Albuquerque I designed, built and tested a staged-recovery rocket,
through eleven design iterations in OpenRocket and CAD. It reached 3,200 feet on its barometric
altimeter and came back under its parachute. The rest of the summer went to the machine shop: FDM
and resin printers, a CNC lathe and mill, and a laser cutter, prototyping parts for autonomous
robotics projects.

## American Rocketry Challenge

In high school I founded and captained our American Rocketry Challenge team, and raised $2,000 from
business sponsors to fund it.
