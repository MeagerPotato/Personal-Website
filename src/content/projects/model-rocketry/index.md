I like rockets that come back.

## Summer 2024

In the summer of 2024 I was an engineer intern with the Air Force Research Laboratory in
Albuquerque, working as a workshop and museum assistant at Explora Science Center and Kirtland Air
Force Base.
As a personal project alongside it I designed, built and tested a staged-recovery rocket through
eleven design iterations. It reached 3,200 feet on its barometric altimeter
and came back under its parachute. The internship itself was the machining workshop and museum
design: researching and building prototypes with FDM and resin printers, laser cutting, and a CNC
lathe and mill.

## American Rocketry Challenge

In high school I founded and captained our American Rocketry Challenge team, and raised $2,000 from
business sponsors to fund it.
