CyberPatriot hands each team a set of deliberately broken computers and a clock. Find the
vulnerabilities, close them, and do not break the services the scenario needs.

I competed all four years as my team's Microsoft Windows specialist. We placed second at the
National Finals twice, in 2025 and 2026 (CyberPatriot 17 had 1,406 teams), and first in the Cisco
Networking Challenge both years.

From 2024 to 2026 I also taught it, as a Windows instructor for middle and high school students in
Troy High School's CyberPatriot program. Along the way I earned the GIAC GFACT certification.
