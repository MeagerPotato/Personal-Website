CyberPatriot hands each team a set of deliberately broken computers and a clock. Find the
vulnerabilities, close them, and do not break the services the scenario needs.

I competed all four years, as my team's Windows specialist and then as its captain. We were
national semifinalist runners-up in 2023 and 2024, then placed second at the National Finals twice,
in 2025 and 2026 (CyberPatriot 17 had 1,406 teams), and first in the Cisco Networking Challenge both
years.

I also taught it: about 200 students over two years, as a program instructor and on the staff of
Troy CyberCamp. Along the way I earned the GIAC GFACT certification.
