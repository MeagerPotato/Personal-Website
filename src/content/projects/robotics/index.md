In 2023 I co-founded my high school's first VEX V5RC team and became its programmer.

## Knowing where you are

A robot on a competition field spends the start of every match on its own, with no one to steer
it. I wrote the navigation in C++: calculus-based odometry and sensor fusion to track where the
robot is, motion profiling to get it somewhere smoothly, and Monte Carlo localization to correct
itself when the wheels slip.

## Growing a program

The one team became seven, with about 60 students and nine national appearances, and we were
National Division Finalists two years running, in 2024 and 2025. Along the way we raised $11,000
through community partnerships and ran a robotics camp for more than 50 students.
