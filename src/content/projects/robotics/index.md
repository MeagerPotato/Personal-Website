In 2023 I co-founded my high school's first VEX team, eleven of us, and became its lead programmer.

## Knowing where you are

A robot on a competition field spends the start of every match on its own, with no one to steer
it. I wrote the navigation in C++: calculus-based odometry and sensor fusion to track where the
robot is, motion profiling to get it somewhere smoothly, and Monte Carlo localization to correct
itself when the wheels slip.

## Growing a program

The one team became three, with 27 members, in 2024, then seven teams and more than 40 members in
2025, along with a robotics summer camp for more than 50 students. As of 2026 the program has
fourteen teams and more than 90 members.

Across the program: four VEX Worlds qualifications, National Division Finalist twice, three California R4
States qualifications, three JROTC Nationals with two JROTC National Division Finals, and National
Build, Create and Inspire Awards.
